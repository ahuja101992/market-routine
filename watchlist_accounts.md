# Watchlist Accounts — Market-Moving Voices

Voices and sources whose statements can move tickers within minutes. Scan for ticker- or sector-relevant content in last 24h on every run. Apply time-bucketing from methodology.

## Political / policy
- @realDonaldTrump (Truth Social — primary source)
- White House press briefings, executive orders
- Treasury Secretary public statements
- US Trade Representative (USTR) — tariff announcements
- Senate Commerce / Banking / Armed Services committee hearings
- House Select Committee on the CCP

## Federal Reserve
- FOMC official statements
- Powell speeches and press conferences
- Regional Fed presidents (esp. NY, Chicago, Atlanta) on scheduled speaking days

## Tech executives
- Elon Musk (X) — TSLA, related names
- Jensen Huang (NVDA) — semis, AI infrastructure
- Sam Altman (OpenAI / X) — AI ecosystem
- Tim Cook (AAPL) — supply chain commentary
- Satya Nadella (MSFT) — cloud / AI infrastructure
- Lisa Su (AMD), Pat Gelsinger / current Intel CEO — semis

## Macro / financial commentators (signal, not noise)
- Major bank research notes (GS, MS, JPM, BofA)
- Druckenmiller, Dalio, Ackman, Burry public statements
- Earnings call commentary from cycle-bellwethers (TSM on semis, GS on banks, WMT on consumer, JPM on credit)

## Geopolitical
- Israel / Iran / Russia / Ukraine / Taiwan headlines
- China MOFCOM / NDRC announcements (export controls, rare earth, retaliatory tariffs)
- OPEC+ meeting outcomes

---
*Curate aggressively. Adding noisy accounts degrades signal quality. If a source generates >10 mentions/week with <10% relevance, remove it.*
