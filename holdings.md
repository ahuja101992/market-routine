# My Holdings

Maintain this file yourself. Add a row every time you buy a stock. Update STATUS to "sold" when you sell.

Format: TICKER | BUY_DATE | BUY_PRICE | SHARES | STATUS | NOTES

## Active positions

TICKER | BUY_DATE | BUY_PRICE | SHARES | STATUS | NOTES
-------|----------|-----------|--------|--------|------
EXAMPLE | 2026-06-04 | 100.00 | 1.0 | active | replace this row with your actual buys

## Sold positions

TICKER | BUY_DATE | BUY_PRICE | SELL_DATE | SELL_PRICE | SHARES | RETURN_PCT | NOTES
-------|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|--------|------------|------
